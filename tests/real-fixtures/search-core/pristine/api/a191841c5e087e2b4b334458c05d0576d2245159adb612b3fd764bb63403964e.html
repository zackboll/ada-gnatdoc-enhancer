Description

Callback-scoped Mat views over caller-owned three-channel UInt16 (CV_16UC3) buffers. Each buffer entry is one complete three-channel pixel. A view never copies or owns the caller's Data: the temporary Mat handed to the callback aliases Data directly and is valid only during the callback. The writable forms let the callback modify Data through the Mat; the read-only forms pass the Mat in mode and accept constant Data. Packed forms describe contiguous row-major or N-D storage; strided forms add row or per-dimension strides counted in complete pixels (not bytes or scalar channel values). Shape, size and stride violations raise OpenCV_Error before the callback is invoked. A temporary view cannot be retained or shallow-copied; use Clone to keep independent values.

Buffer_Array

type Buffer_Array is array (Natural range <>) of UInt16_Vec3.Vector;

A flat buffer of UInt16_Vec3.Vector elements supplied by the caller. Only Data'First (the first element) and Data'Length (the capacity, in complete pixels) are used.

With_Read_Only_Mat_View

procedure With_Read_Only_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : Mat))

Read-only view of a packed N-dimensional buffer, with the geometry rules of the writable N-dimensional overload.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned packed buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0).

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape is invalid, has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, or Data'Length differs from the product of Shape.

With_Read_Only_Strided_Mat_View

procedure With_Read_Only_Strided_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Strides : Dimension_Stride_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : Mat))

Read-only view of a strided N-dimensional buffer, following the geometry, stride and capacity rules of the writable strided N-dimensional overload. Strides count complete pixels, not bytes.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant. Its length must be at least Shape (first) * Strides (first).

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0).

Strides

One stride per dimension, in complete pixels; the final stride must be 1 and each outer stride must cover its nested inner block.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape or Strides violates the rules above, Shape has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, or Data is too short.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

With_Writable_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable packed N-dimensional CV_16UC3 Mat that aliases Data; the final dimension varies fastest. Shape shall have 2 to 32 (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0) positive extents and Data'Length shall equal their product.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned packed buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must equal the product of Shape.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0), in OpenCV dimension order.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape has fewer than 2 or more than 32 dimensions (more than 10 on OpenCV 5.0), more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, an extent is zero, or Data'Length differs from the product of Shape.
