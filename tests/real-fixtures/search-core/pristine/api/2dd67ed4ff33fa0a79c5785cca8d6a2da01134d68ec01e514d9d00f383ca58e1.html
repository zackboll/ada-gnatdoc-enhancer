Description

Callback-scoped Mat views over caller-owned two-channel UInt8 (CV_8UC2) buffers. A view never copies or owns the caller's Data: the temporary Mat handed to the callback aliases Data directly and is valid only during the callback. Each Data entry is one complete OpenCV.Core.UInt8_Vec2.Vector (one pixel). The writable forms let the callback modify Data through the Mat; the read-only forms pass the Mat in mode and accept constant Data. Packed forms describe contiguous row-major or N-D storage; strided forms add row or per-dimension strides counted in complete pixels (not scalar channels or bytes). Shape, size and stride violations raise OpenCV_Error before the callback is invoked. A temporary view cannot be retained, shallow-copied, sliced, reshaped or used as a destination; use Clone to keep independent values.

Buffer_Array

type Buffer_Array is array (Natural range <>) of UInt8_Vec2.Vector;

A flat buffer of UInt8_Vec2.Vector pixels supplied by the caller. Its index bounds are the caller's choice: only Data'First (the first pixel) and Data'Length (the capacity, counted in pixels) are used, and the lower bound is not exposed through the Mat.

With_Read_Only_Mat_View

procedure With_Read_Only_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : Mat))

Read-only view of a packed N-dimensional buffer, with the geometry rules of the writable N-dimensional overload: Shape has 2 to 32 (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0) positive extents and Data'Length equals their product. The temporary Image is valid only during the callback.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned packed buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, in OpenCV dimension order.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape is invalid, has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, or Data'Length differs from the product of Shape.

With_Read_Only_Mat_View

procedure With_Read_Only_Mat_View
  (Data          : aliased Buffer_Array;
   Rows, Columns : Positive;
   Process       : not null access procedure (Image : Mat))

Read-only view of a packed row-major buffer; the geometry and capacity rules are those of the corresponding writable overload, so Data'Length must equal Rows * Columns. Aliased constant Data is accepted without copying. The temporary Image is valid only during the callback.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant. Its length must be Rows * Columns pixels.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

Positive column count of Image.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Data'Length differs from Rows * Columns or a dimension exceeds the native range.

With_Read_Only_Strided_Mat_View

procedure With_Read_Only_Strided_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Strides : Dimension_Stride_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : Mat))

Read-only view of a strided N-dimensional buffer, following the geometry, stride and capacity rules of the writable strided N-dimensional overload. Strides count complete pixels, not scalar channels or bytes. The temporary Image is valid only during the callback.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant. Its length must be at least Shape (first) * Strides (first) pixels.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0).

Strides

One stride per dimension, in pixels; the final stride must be 1 and each outer stride must cover its nested inner block.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape or Strides violates the rules above, Shape has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, or Data is too short.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant. Its length must be at least Rows * Row_Stride pixels.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

Positive column count of Image.

Row_Stride

Distance, in pixels, between row starts; at least Columns.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

With_Writable_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable packed N-dimensional CV_8UC2 Mat that aliases Data. Shape'Length must be in 2 .. 32 (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0) and every extent must be positive; Shape iteration order maps to OpenCV dimension order regardless of Shape'First. Data'Length must equal product (Shape) exactly, counted in pixels. Storage is packed and continuous with the final dimension varying fastest. Callback lifetime and escape rules match the Rows/Columns overload.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned packed buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must equal the product of Shape, in pixels.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0), in OpenCV dimension order.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape has fewer than 2 or more than 32 dimensions (more than 10 on OpenCV 5.0), more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, an extent is zero, or Data'Length differs from the product of Shape.

With_Writable_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Mat_View
  (Data          : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Rows, Columns : Positive;
   Process       : not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable CV_8UC2 Mat that aliases Data. Image (Row, Column) is Data (Data'First + Row * Columns + Column). Data'Length must equal Rows * Columns; it counts pixels, not scalar channels. Strides count complete vectors. Shallow escape is prohibited; Clone may escape.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must be Rows * Columns pixels.

Rows

Positive row count of Image, within the native signed 32-bit range.

Columns

Positive column count of Image, within the native signed 32-bit range.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Data'Length differs from Rows * Columns or a dimension exceeds the native range.

With_Writable_Strided_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Strided_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Strides : Dimension_Stride_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable strided N-dimensional CV_8UC2 Mat that aliases Data. Shape'Length must be in 2 .. 32 (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0) with positive extents, and Strides supplies exactly one stride per dimension in the same order. Strides count complete pixels, not scalar channels and not bytes. The final stride must be 1 and each outer stride must cover its nested inner block: Strides (I) >= Strides (I + 1) * Shape (I + 1). Data'Length must be at least Shape (first) * Strides (first) pixels. Callback lifetime and escape rules match the other overloads.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must be at least Shape (first) * Strides (first) pixels.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0).

Strides

One stride per dimension, in pixels (not scalar channels or bytes); the final stride must be 1 and each outer stride must cover its nested inner block.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape or Strides violates the rules above, Shape has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, the two arrays differ in length, or Data is too short.

With_Writable_Strided_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Strided_Mat_View
  (Data                      : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Rows, Columns, Row_Stride : Positive;
   Process                   :
     not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable CV_8UC2 Mat that aliases Data with Row_Stride complete pixels between logical row starts. Row_Stride must be at least Columns. Image (Row, Column) maps to Data (Data'First + Row * Row_Stride + Column). Data'Length must be at least Rows * Row_Stride, including padding after the final logical row. Padding is outside the logical Mat. Ownership, callback lifetime and escape rules match With_Writable_Mat_View.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must be at least Rows * Row_Stride pixels.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

Positive column count of Image.

Row_Stride

Distance, in pixels, between the starts of consecutive rows; at least Columns.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Row_Stride is less than Columns, Data is shorter than Rows * Row_Stride, or a dimension exceeds the native range.
