Description

Callback-scoped Mat views over caller-owned four-channel Int32 (CV_32SC4) buffers. One buffer element is one complete four-channel pixel. A view never copies or owns the caller's Data: the temporary Mat handed to the callback aliases Data directly and is valid only during the callback. The writable forms let the callback modify Data through the Mat; the read-only forms pass the Mat in mode and accept constant Data. Packed forms describe contiguous row-major or N-D storage; strided forms add row or per-dimension strides counted in complete vectors (not bytes). Shape, size and stride violations raise OpenCV_Error before the callback is invoked. A temporary view cannot be retained or shallow-copied; use Clone to keep independent values.

Buffer_Array

type Buffer_Array is array (Natural range <>) of Int32_Vec4.Vector;

A flat buffer of Int32_Vec4.Vector elements supplied by the caller. Its index bounds are the caller's choice: only Data'First (the first element) and Data'Length (the capacity) are used. Caller owns storage; strides count complete vectors. Shallow escape is prohibited; Clone owns independent storage.

With_Read_Only_Mat_View

procedure With_Read_Only_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : Mat))

Read-only view of a packed N-dimensional buffer, with the geometry rules of the writable N-dimensional overload. The temporary Image is valid only during the callback.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned packed buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, in OpenCV dimension order.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape is invalid, has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, or Data'Length differs from the product of Shape.

Read-only view of a packed row-major buffer. Data may be an aliased constant, and Data'Length must equal Rows * Columns. The temporary Image is valid only during the callback.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant. Its length must be Rows * Columns.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

Positive column count of Image.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Data'Length differs from Rows * Columns or a dimension exceeds the native range.

With_Read_Only_Strided_Mat_View

procedure With_Read_Only_Strided_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Strides : Dimension_Stride_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : Mat))

Read-only view of a strided N-dimensional buffer, following the geometry, stride and capacity rules of the writable strided N-dimensional overload. Strides count complete vectors, not bytes. The temporary Image is valid only during the callback.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant. Its length must be at least Shape (first) * Strides (first).

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0).

Strides

One stride per dimension, in complete four-channel elements; the final stride must be 1 and each outer stride must cover its nested inner block.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary read-only Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape or Strides violates the rules above, Shape has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, or Data is too short.

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases; it may be an aliased constant. Its length must be at least Rows * Row_Stride.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

Positive column count of Image.

Row_Stride

Distance, in complete four-channel elements, between row starts; at least Columns.

Process

With_Writable_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Mat_View
  (Data    : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Shape   : Dimension_Array;
   Process : not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable packed N-dimensional CV_32SC4 Mat that aliases Data. Shape has 2 to 32 (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0) positive extents in OpenCV dimension order, the final dimension varies fastest, and Data'Length must equal the product of Shape. No data is copied.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned packed buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must equal the product of Shape.

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0), in OpenCV dimension order.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape has fewer than 2 or more than 32 dimensions (more than 10 on OpenCV 5.0), more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, an extent is zero, or Data'Length differs from the product of Shape.

With_Writable_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Mat_View
  (Data          : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Rows, Columns : Positive;
   Process       : not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable CV_32SC4 Mat that aliases Data. Image (Row, Column) is Data (Data'First + Row * Columns + Column). Writes through Image immediately modify Data and writes through Data immediately affect Image. No data is copied.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must be Rows * Columns.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

Positive column count of Image.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Data'Length differs from Rows * Columns or a dimension exceeds the native range.

With_Writable_Strided_Mat_View

  (Data    : aliased in out Buffer_Array;

Invokes Process with a temporary writable strided N-dimensional CV_32SC4 Mat that aliases Data. Strides count complete vectors, not bytes; the final stride must be 1 and each outer stride must cover its nested inner block. Data'Length must be at least Shape (first) * Strides (first). No data is copied.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must be at least Shape (first) * Strides (first).

Shape

Extent of each dimension, 2 to 32 positive values (at most 10 on OpenCV 5.0).

Strides

One stride per dimension, in complete four-channel elements (not bytes); the final stride must be 1 and each outer stride must cover its nested inner block.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Shape or Strides violates the rules above, Shape has more dimensions than the linked OpenCV supports, the two arrays differ in length, or Data is too short.

With_Writable_Strided_Mat_View

procedure With_Writable_Strided_Mat_View
  (Data                      : aliased in out Buffer_Array;
   Rows, Columns, Row_Stride : Positive;
   Process                   :
     not null access procedure (Image : in out Mat))

Invokes Process with a temporary writable CV_32SC4 Mat that aliases Data with Row_Stride vectors between logical row starts. Row_Stride must be at least Columns, and Data'Length must be at least Rows * Row_Stride. Padding is outside the logical Mat. No data is copied.

Parameters
Data

The caller-owned buffer that Image aliases, passed by reference; its length must be at least Rows * Row_Stride.

Rows

Positive row count of Image.

Columns

Positive column count of Image.

Row_Stride

Distance, in complete four-channel elements, between the starts of consecutive rows; at least Columns.

Process

Callback that receives the temporary writable Image. It is valid only during the call and must not be retained.

Raised exceptions
OpenCV_Error

Raised, before Process is invoked, when Row_Stride is less than Columns, Data is shorter than Rows * Row_Stride, or a dimension exceeds the native range.
